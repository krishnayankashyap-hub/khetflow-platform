# 🧅 The “Two Onions”/Any Harvest Problem

*Imagine standing in a bustling vegetable market. Before you sit **two onions**:*

<br/>

| 🧅 The First Onion | 🧅 The Second Onion |
| :---: | :---: |
| Perfectly round, polished, and flawless. | Slightly misshapen, uneven, with visible roots. |
| **You instinctively choose this one.** | **What happens to this one?** |

</div>

<br/>

Even though the second onion is **100% edible and nutritious**, it gets brutally rejected by the traditional supply chain simply because it fails to meet superficial cosmetic standards. 

Farmers are forced to sell it for pennies — or worse, let it rot in landfills. This doesn't just create methane emissions; it fuels a massive national crisis:

> ### 🚨 India’s ₹90,000+ Crore annual agricultural waste problem.

Months of back-breaking labor, financial investment, and hope are wiped out in seconds by appearance-based grading systems controlled by exploitative middlemen. 

This rejected produce doesn't just mean lost vegetables. It directly causes:
- 📉 **Crippled farmer income**
- 💔 **Broken family stability**
- 🚫 **Denied loan eligibility**

<br/>

#### ✨ Mixed Batch AR Scanner
Farmers simply capture **one image** of a mixed crate. Our AI instantly:
- 🔍 **Detects** every single produce item in the frame.
- 📍 **Maps** X/Y coordinates for precision tracking.
- 📱 **Applies** AR overlay markers on the screen.
- 📊 **Generates** instant inventory classification (e.g., 🟢 Grade B, 🔴 Grade C).

*This dramatically reduces manual sorting time, establishes strict quality specifications for buyers, and increases listing efficiency.*

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Travel%20and%20places/Delivery%20Truck.png" alt="Delivery Truck" width="28" height="28" /> 3. The Logistics Portal (AI “Milk-Run” Routing)

Transportation is the biggest financial hurdle for small farmers, often making it too expensive to sell small batches of crops. **KhetFlow solves this using AI-optimized transport pooling.**

**How it works (The "Half-Empty" Example):** 
If a commercial truck or local tempo is driving from Point A to Point C half-empty, our AI dynamically routes it to pick up small 50kg crates from farmers at Point B along the way. 

This allows drivers to monetize their empty space, while farmers get their produce delivered without having to rent an entire truck.

<div align="center">

| 📍 Step 1 | 📍 Step 2 | 📍 Step 3 | 📍 Step 4 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Finds half-empty trucks** locally | **Groups nearby farmers** needing transport | **Creates smart pickup routes** along the way | **Delivers directly** to B2B buyers |

</div>

> **🌱 Result:** Drivers make extra money, fuel waste is reduced, and **farmers save over 60% on shipping costs** through shared logistics!
